# Income Tax Law 2009 Guide (Tax System)

> 🇦🇫 A comprehensive open-source guide explaining the wage withholding tax applications under the Afghanistan Income Tax Law 2009, designed to help developers and businesses comply with local tax regulations.

## 🚀 Why this project matters
Navigating Afghan tax law can be complex, especially for developers building payroll, HR, or accounting software. This repository provides a clear, structured, and accessible reference for the wage withholding tax rules, including exact brackets, calculation examples, and legal distinctions (e.g., resident vs. non-resident, employee vs. independent contractor). It serves as a foundational resource for anyone digitizing Afghan financial compliance.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Clear Withholding Brackets**: Detailed rate tables for Monthly, Semi-Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Weekly, and Daily payrolls.
- **Practical Calculation Examples**: Step-by-step math examples (e.g., calculating tax for a AFN 20,000 monthly salary).
- **Legal Distinctions**: Clear guidelines on Employee vs. Independent Contractor and Resident vs. Non-Resident status.
- **Compliance Guidelines**: Outlines reporting deadlines (within 10 days of month-end) and enforcement provisions.
- **Official References**: Cites specific legal documents (e.g., Article 58, Public Ruling 1384/6) for verification.

## 💡 Usage for Developers
Developers building payroll systems in Afghanistan can use this guide to:
1. Implement accurate tax-bracket logic in their backend systems.
2. Validate withholding calculations against the provided examples.
3. Ensure their software prompts users for correct residency and employment-type classifications.
